# Limitations

## Traditional symbolic framework

BaZi is a traditional symbolic framework. Deterministic software output does not make personality interpretations, future events, or life outcomes scientifically established.

## School variation

Different BaZi schools can use different conventions for year boundaries, day rollover, true-solar time, hidden stems, seasonal strength, and Luck Pillars. The evidence applies only to the school profile and engine version recorded in each snapshot.

## Boundary sensitivity

Approximate city coordinates, historical civil-time data, the equation-of-time approximation, and dates close to a solar-term or Pillar boundary can affect results. The public vectors intentionally include time and date boundary examples.

## Unknown time

An unknown birth time produces Three Pillars. The Hour Pillar remains unknown. Hour-dependent interpretation must be omitted or marked conditional.

## Coordinates

The calculation uses the selected city's coordinates, not a precise birth-building coordinate. People born far from the city center or close to a true-solar boundary should treat the result as boundary-sensitive.

## Five Element weights

The `seasonal-v1` Five Element percentages are a disclosed interpretive weighting model. They are not physical measurements, medical data, psychological assessment, or evidence of future outcomes.

## Decision boundary

This material must not replace medical, legal, financial, psychological, relationship, or other qualified professional advice.
